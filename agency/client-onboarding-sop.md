# Agency SOP: client onboarding checklist (missed-call text-back + Voice AI)

A checklist for agencies setting up phone automation for local service businesses. Adapt to your tools. Results vary by business; do not promise clients specific outcomes.

## 0. Before the sale
- [ ] Ask for the client's own call numbers (calls/week, share missed, average job value). Use the [missed call calculator](https://getpeacecall.com/missed-call-calculator/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=agency-templates) with *their* numbers, labeled as an estimate.
- [ ] Compare options honestly: AI receptionist, answering service, voicemail. [Answering service cost estimator](https://getpeacecall.com/answering-service-cost-estimator/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=agency-templates).
- [ ] Written scope: what you set up, what the client must provide, monthly costs they pay (software, A2P, per-segment SMS, voice minutes).

## 1. Kickoff (day 1)
- [ ] Legal business name, EIN, DBA, address, website, authorized contact for A2P.
- [ ] Business hours, service area ZIPs, services offered and NOT offered, approved fees, emergency policy, on-call roster.
- [ ] Owner-approved safety lines for the trade.
- [ ] Access: client invites you as a user; never ask for their passwords or card details by chat/email.

## 2. Compliance (days 1–3)
- [ ] Website has Privacy Policy, Terms, consent checkboxes ([A2P checklist](../a2p/a2p-10dlc-registration-checklist.md)).
- [ ] Submit A2P 10DLC brand + campaign under the client's own business. Fees start on submission: client approves first.
- [ ] Check state quiet-hours and consent rules for their state.

## 3. Build (days 2–7, while A2P is pending)
- [ ] Phone number + call forwarding; missed-call trigger → wait ~10s → send text-back ([templates](../sms/missed-call-text-back-en.md), [Spanish](../sms/missed-call-text-back-es.md)) → create contact/opportunity → notify owner.
- [ ] One follow-up max if no reply. STOP handled automatically.
- [ ] Voice AI or answering-service script ([prompts](../voice-ai/voice-ai-prompt-examples.md), [after-hours scripts](../after-hours/answering-scripts-by-trade.md)); knowledge base loaded.
- [ ] Calendar + booking only if the client wants live booking.

## 4. Go-live (after A2P approval)
- [ ] Number linked to campaign and shows A2P Verified.
- [ ] Real-phone tests: missed call → text arrives; emergency wording; transfer to on-call and backup; outside hours.
- [ ] Owner signs off on scripts and texts.
- [ ] Record a baseline week of call numbers before changes.

## 5. Weekly review
- [ ] Calls, missed calls, texts sent, replies, bookings (client's real numbers only).
- [ ] Read Voice AI transcripts; fix prompt issues; never change safety lines without owner approval.
- [ ] Check A2P status, opt-outs, delivery errors (e.g. 30034).
- [ ] Short written report to the client.

## 6. Offboarding
- [ ] Export contacts and conversation history for the client; transfer or release numbers per their wishes; remove your access.
