## Ground rules for using these templates
- Edit every bracket. Read every message before it reaches a customer.
- Only text people who contacted you or gave consent; honor STOP right away; respect quiet hours.
- Register A2P 10DLC before texting from a US 10-digit number.
- Safety lines (gas, smoke, sparks, CO, 911) come first and must be approved by the business owner.
- These are starting points, not promises of results.
