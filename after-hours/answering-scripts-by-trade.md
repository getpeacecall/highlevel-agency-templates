For a person, an answering service or an AI receptionist. **Safety lines first**, approved by the owner. General starting point, not safety, legal or medical advice.

## Core flow (all trades)
Greeting → safety check → name + callback (read back) → service address/ZIP → problem in caller's words → urgency → escalate, book or take callback → fee disclosure (if any) → recap → text confirmation.

**Greeting:** "Thanks for calling [Business]. This is [Name / the virtual assistant]. How can I help?"

**Recap:** "Just to confirm: [name], [callback], [address], [summary], [next step]. Is that correct?"

**Fee line (only if you charge one, before dispatch):** "Our after-hours service call fee is [amount], and it [is / is not] credited toward the repair."

**After-hours text:** "Thanks for calling [Business]. We're closed now and open at [8am]. Reply with what you need and we'll text first thing."

## Universal safety triage
| If the caller mentions | Say, then stop intake and escalate |
|---|---|
| Gas smell, CO alarm, smoke | "Please leave the building now and call 911 or your gas utility from outside." |
| Sparks, burning smell, downed line | "Stay away from it and call 911." |
| Active flooding / sewage backup | "If it is safe, shut off the main water valve. I'm paging our on-call technician now." |
| Danger to a person, chest pain | "If anyone is in danger, please hang up and call 911 now." |

## HVAC
Ask one at a time: heating, cooling or both? What is the system doing? Type (furnace, boiler, heat pump, central AC, mini-split) and age if known. Anyone vulnerable at home (infant, elderly, medical)? Indoor temperature? How long down? Water leaking or burning smell (not gas)?
- Level 1 (gas/CO): safety line, stop. Level 2 (no heat in freezing weather with vulnerable occupant): page on-call. Level 3–4: book next priority/standard slot.

## Plumbing
Active leak right now? Water near electrical? No water at all? Sewer backup? Main valve location known? Fuel appliance involved (water heater) → same gas/CO rules.
- Full script: [Plumbing after-hours script](https://getpeacecall.com/plumbing-after-hours-answering-script/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=agency-templates)

## Electrical
Sparks, smoke, burning smell, hot outlet, downed line → 911 line, stop. Otherwise: whole-home or one circuit out? Breaker tripping? Utility outage in the area? Medical equipment depending on power?
- Full script: [Electrician after-hours script](https://getpeacecall.com/electrician-after-hours-answering-script/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=agency-templates)

## Roofing
Water coming in now? Storm damage, leak or new-roof quote? Photos possible? Do not advise anyone to go on the roof; suggest a bucket and moving belongings if safe.

## Dental
New or existing patient? Severe swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing → 911/ER line. No clinical advice by phone; take callback for the on-call dentist.

## Law firm
"Please don't share case details in a message." Collect name, callback, best time, matter type in one word. No legal advice; no promises about the case.

## Never say
No diagnosing, no unapproved prices, no improvised safety advice, no promised arrival times, no pretending to be human, no collecting card numbers or passwords.

## Before go-live
Test on a real phone: gas/CO wording, vulnerable-occupant case, routine booking, outside service area, price question, request for a human, silence, wrong number, during vs outside hours. Read transcripts daily for the first week.
