## More general variants

1. Hi, this is [Business]. Sorry we missed your call! Is this about a service request? Reply here and someone will get back to you shortly.
2. Thanks for calling [Business]. We're with another customer right now. What do you need help with? Reply STOP to opt out.
3. Hi from [Business]! We missed you. Want to book a visit? Pick a time: [Link]
4. [Business] here. Sorry we couldn't pick up. Reply with your address and a short description and we'll follow up today.
5. Hi! You just called [Business]. Is it urgent? Reply URGENT and we'll prioritize your call.
6. Thanks for reaching out to [Business]. Our hours are [Hours]. Text us anytime and we'll respond first thing.
7. Missed your call, sorry! Reply YES to get a call back within [X] minutes.
8. Hi, [Business] here. Want a quote? Send a photo and a short description by text.

## Rules
- First text is a reply to the caller, not a promotion. Add "Reply STOP to opt out" on the first message.
- Send at most one follow-up if there is no reply.
- Never ask for card numbers, medical or legal details by text.
