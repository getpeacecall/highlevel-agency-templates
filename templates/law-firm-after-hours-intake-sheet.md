# Law firm after-hours intake sheet (callback-first)

A one-page sheet for an answering service, receptionist, or AI voice agent taking after-hours calls for a small law firm. It collects only what the firm needs to call back and run a conflict check, following the approach in ABA Formal Opinion 510 (limit intake to what's reasonably necessary, and caution callers not to volunteer more). Have a responsible attorney adapt it to your practice areas and your state's rules. This is not legal advice.

Source guide: https://getpeacecall.com/law-firm-after-hours-answering/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=social

## Say first

- "Our office is closed, but I can take a message for the legal team. I'm not an attorney and can't give legal advice."
- "Are you or anyone else in immediate danger?" If yes: "Please hang up and call 911."
- "Please don't share details about what happened yet. An attorney first needs to check whether the firm can help."

## Fields

| # | Field | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Caller name | |
| 2 | Callback number | |
| 3 | OK to call / text / leave a message at that number? | Call Y/N · Text Y/N · Message Y/N |
| 4 | Current client? If yes, attorney's name | |
| 5 | Type of matter, in a few words | |
| 6 | Names of other people or companies involved (for conflict check) | |
| 7 | Court date, hearing, or deadline, and the date | |
| 8 | Best time to call back | |

Do **not** record the story of what happened. If the caller starts describing facts: "Thank you. I'm going to stop you there so the attorney can review this properly first."

## Close

"Someone from the firm will contact you [during business hours / by (time)]. Taking your message doesn't mean the firm has agreed to represent you."

## Routing (agree with the attorneys before launch)

| Signal | Next step | Notify |
|---|---|---|
| Danger, hurt, threatened | 911 and the National Domestic Violence Hotline, 1-800-799-7233 | On-call attorney or office manager, per policy |
| Person in custody | Name, facility if known, callback number | On-call attorney, if the firm offers after-hours response |
| Deadline inside your window | Record the date, call back first thing | Responsible attorney or intake lead |
| Current client | Name and attorney's name | That attorney or their assistant |
| New matter, no deadline | Book a callback | Intake team, next business day |

## Rules to review first

ABA Model Rule 1.18 (prospective clients), Rule 1.6(c) (confidentiality), Rule 5.3 (nonlawyer assistance), Formal Opinion 510 (2024), Formal Opinion 512 (2024, generative AI), plus your state's versions.
