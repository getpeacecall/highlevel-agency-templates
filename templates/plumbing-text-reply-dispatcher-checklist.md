# Plumbing text-reply dispatcher checklist

Use this when a caller replies to your missed-call text. Work top to bottom, one message at a time, and move on only after a reply. Replace anything in [brackets] and have your team approve the safety lines before use.

Condensed from Peace Call's plumber text-back guide:
https://getpeacecall.com/missed-call-text-back-plumbers/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=social

## Step 1. Sort (emergencies first)

| If the reply mentions | Send | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gas, smell gas | `If you smell gas, leave the home now. Don't use switches or phones inside. Call 911 or your gas utility from outside. We'll follow up once you're safe.` | Stop intake. Alert on-call. |
| URGENT, leak, burst, flood, sewage | `Thanks. If you can do it safely, close the main water shutoff and stay away from outlets or anything electrical near the water. What's the address?` | Go to step 2, then 5b. |
| anything else | (go to step 2) | |

## Step 2. Address

`Thanks. What's the service address and ZIP?`

Outside your area: `Sorry, [ZIP] is outside our service area, so we can't send a tech. We'd suggest calling another local plumber.`

## Step 3. Water status

`Is the water off right now? Reply YES, NO, or NOT SURE.`

If unclear and water might still be running, treat it as urgent.

## Step 4. Details

`Where is the problem (kitchen, bath, basement, outside), and anything we need to get in, like a gate code or pets?`

Optional: `If it's easy, text a photo of the leak or the label on the water heater. It helps the tech bring the right parts.` (Photos arrive as MMS, which costs more than SMS.)

Optional: `Thanks. Do you own the home or rent? If you rent, please let your landlord or property manager know too.`

## Step 5a. Book (routine)

`Got it. We have [Tue 8-12] or [Wed 1-5]. Which works better?`

`You're booked for [day, window]. We'll text when the tech is on the way. Reply here if anything changes.`

## Step 5b. Hand off (urgent)

`Thanks. [Name], our on-call plumber, will call you shortly from [number]. If water reaches outlets or wiring, stay out of that area.`

## Handoff note (copy into the CRM before the tech rolls)

```text
Name:
Service address + ZIP:
Problem type (leak / clog / water heater / sewage / other):
Water off? (yes / no / not sure):
Where in the home:
Owner or renter:
Access notes (gate code, pets):
Photo received? (yes / no):
Booked window or on-call callback time:
```

## Rules

- Don't diagnose or quote by text. Only quote a fee if it is a fixed, approved number in writing.
- Promise a call back, not an arrival time.
- Never ask for card numbers by SMS.
- Someone must own the replies after hours, or drop the URGENT keyword from your first text.
