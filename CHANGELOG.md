# Changelog

- 2026-10-31: Added `templates/hvac-after-hours-call-log.csv`: a spreadsheet-ready log for after-hours HVAC calls with the fields worth capturing on every call (confirmed callback, address and ZIP, caller type, system and symptom, triage level 1 to 4, vulnerable occupants, after-hours fee disclosed, disposition), plus a short level guide row. Condensed from Peace Call's HVAC after-hours answering script: https://getpeacecall.com/hvac-after-hours-answering-script/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=social . Not safety or legal advice; set your own thresholds and fee policy. Prepared with AI assistance; Peace Call is a HighLevel affiliate.

- 2026-10-30: Added `templates/jobber-highlevel-monthly-cost-sheet.csv`: a spreadsheet-ready list of the published Jobber and HighLevel prices a home service business is most likely to pay (plans, extra users, AI Receptionist, phone number, SMS, A2P, Voice AI), each with the date it was checked and empty columns for your own quantities and monthly total. Prices come from each company's pricing page and help center as of October 8, 2026; check them again before you decide. Prepared with AI assistance; Peace Call is a HighLevel affiliate.

- 2026-10-28: Added `templates/law-firm-after-hours-intake-sheet.md`: a callback-first, one-page intake sheet for after-hours law firm calls (eight fields, a stop-the-story line, and an urgent-routing table), following the limited-intake approach in ABA Formal Opinion 510. Not legal advice.

- 2026-10-27: Added `worksheets/ai-employee-break-even-worksheet.md`: a monthly per-location worksheet for deciding between HighLevel AI Employee Growth ($50) and Unlimited ($97), with the break-even formula and two reference points (about 883 minutes at $0.06/min, about 319 with ElevenLabs V3). Rates as published in October 2026.

- 2026-10-26: Added `missed-call-text-back/manual-callback-and-follow-up-texts.csv`: five texts for returning a missed call by hand and for a single follow-up when there's no reply, with character counts and usage notes. Bracketed placeholders count toward length until replaced.

- 2026-10-25: Added `trial/14-day-trial-cost-tracker.md`: a fill-in sheet for HighLevel's 14-day trial covering key dates, one-time and monthly charges, a daily wallet usage log, cost per test call, and the cancel steps.

- 2026-10-24: Added `voice-ai/salon-med-spa-intake-fields.md`: what a salon or med spa AI receptionist should collect on the call, what to leave for staff or secure forms, one urgent-triage question, and five launch tests.

- 2026-10-22: Added `voice-ai/hvac-plumbing-intake-fields.md`: 13 CRM fields for HVAC and plumbing calls, a four-level triage table with placeholder safety lines, a HighLevel Voice AI action map, and seven launch tests.

- 2026-10-21: Added `voice-ai/voice-ai-trial-test-log.md`: a one-agent trial log with 15 test scenarios, a Web Call vs Phone Call column for transfers, and an after-test cost check.

- 2026-10-20: Added `voice-ai/roofing-storm-mode-prompt.md`: a storm-season roofing prompt with a four-level triage, insurance-aware intake fields, a never-do list and seven test calls.

- 2026-10-19: Added `voice-ai/dental-scheduling-assistant-prompt.md`: a scheduling-only dental prompt with urgent-call rules, a do-not-collect list and BAA questions to answer first.
